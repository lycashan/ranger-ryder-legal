Ranger Ryder YouTube Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Ranger Ryder YouTube Uploader is a private, personal automation tool built and used solely by Billy Ryder to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channel, Ranger Ryder. It is not distributed to the public and does not have other users.

What it does

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload video files that Billy Ryder has already created, and to set their titles, descriptions, and playlists on his own channel.

What data it accesses

It accesses only the YouTube channel it is authorized against (Billy Ryder's own Ranger Ryder channel), using the youtube.force-ssl scope, solely to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What data it stores

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the developer's own computer and are never shared with any third party. No data belonging to other people is collected, stored, or processed by this tool.
